# Illustration de l’accueil public

Image originale générée avec l’outil ImageGen intégré le 25 octobre 2026.
Elle sert de décor à l’accueil et ne représente pas des membres de la plateforme.

Fichiers livrés :

- `public/images/home/services-proximite-1920.webp` : 1920 × 960 pixels.
- `public/images/home/services-proximite-960.webp` : 960 × 480 pixels.

Les fichiers WebP sont des versions redimensionnées et encodées à partir du
PNG généré, sans modification de la scène. La page choisit la résolution avec
`srcset`. L’image est décorative (`alt` vide) et chargée prioritairement.
Le dégradé de contraste et le cadrage mobile sont définis en CSS.

## Prompt utilisé

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: original website hero background photograph for PROKEJEM, a local services marketplace. Create a wide panoramic landscape photo, approximately 2:1. Scene: a welcoming real contemporary home with off-white plaster walls, warm wood and a discreet houseplant, soft daylight. On the RIGHT HALF, a woman craftsperson with medium brown skin in simple navy work clothes and an older Black male homeowner in casual neutral clothes are finishing the installation of a small wooden shelf together. Their natural relaxed expressions convey competence and friendly everyday help; they look at the shelf rather than posing at the camera. Realistic hands, tools and body proportions, candid editorial photography, natural skin texture. IMPORTANT COMPOSITION: keep the LEFT 45 percent as a quiet, softly lit pale wall with very few details, for readable website headline and buttons. Keep the two people's heads and the activity in the upper-right and middle-right area so they remain visible above a foreground search panel along the bottom of the webpage. The room feels authentic and lived in, not a showroom; contemporary, inclusive, warm. No text, logos, watermarks, badges or interface. This is a photographic illustration, not a testimonial or an actual marketplace profile.
